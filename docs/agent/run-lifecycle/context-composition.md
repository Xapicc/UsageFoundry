# Context composition

[← run-lifecycle index](../run-lifecycle.md)

Read before editing `contextComposition` or `parseComposition` in `src/lib/contextPruning.ts`, or the `context_compositions` table.

**The tick reads what the window is *made of* as well as how full it is, and the two are not one measurement.** `contextComposition` spawns `winnow context --depth 3 --json` beside `sampleContext` on the same tick, and stores one row per provenance in `context_compositions` — tool traffic, prefix, retained reasoning, standing configuration, conversation, and the residual winnow calls `unattributed`. It exists because every other figure on this path is a *size*: how full the window is, how much a cut would take out, what the cut would cost. None of them says what the window is growing **on**, so an operator watching a run climb to the ceiling could see the climb and had no way to tell whether it was tool output a prune would take, a prefix a prune cannot touch, or retained reasoning that is neither — three cases with three different remedies behind one line. Five things about it are decisions rather than tuning. It is **gated on `pruningEnabled()`**, on `observePlan`'s rule and for its reason rather than by analogy: read-only is not the same as permitted, and an operator who switched the feature off has asked that winnow is not spawned against their conversation — where `sampleContext` beside it stays ungated because it spawns nothing. It is read **above** the ceiling comparison rather than below it, because "what is this growing on" is worth answering at 60,000 tokens and not only at the 200,000 where the app starts acting; below the `continue` it would exist only for conversations already past the point where anything cheap can be done. Its pacing is its own constant, `COMPOSITION_REMEASURE_GROWTH_TOKENS` at 40,000 against the ceiling's 25,000, on its own map — `ceilingMeasuredAt` paces a *decision* where being late costs money on every turn between, this paces a *picture* where being late costs a point on a graph, and one mark for both would tie the picture's cadence to a policy constant that moves for reasons of its own. And that distance is **absolute** where the ceiling's is one-sided: a prune drops the conversation by tens of thousands of tokens, so a one-sided test would leave the whole post-cut shape unread until the run had grown back — the one moment the composition is worth having. **The distance paces growth; the cut clears the mark itself.** It has to, because a cut is not obliged to move the sampled figure at all: under the fork engine it does not move it — the API window after the resume was 1,153 to 5,238 tokens *higher* than before the cut on all five forks measured here — and under the in-place engine 12 of this install's 54 receipts removed under 40,000 tokens. Left to the distance alone, every one of those left the stack drawing the shape the conversation had before the cut, and a cut that lowered the figure had to regrow what it took *and then* 40,000 more before anything re-read it. `forgetComposition` is called where a conversation is cut or replaced and never from the tick, which cannot see either: the ceiling watcher clears it as it writes the interrupt, which is the only route to an early-end cut, `pruneAtBoundary` clears its own because a natural boundary never passes through the watcher, and the fresh-start branch in `startRun` clears it because dropping the whole conversation is not a cut and passes neither of the other two. Both engines, both moments, and the restart. The distance does not cover the restart either: `freshStartContextTokens` is floored at 20,000, so a threshold of 80,000 restarts a conversation last read near 85k at roughly the 55k of system prompt, tool list and task, a 30k move that leaves the stack drawing the replaced conversation until the new one has moved 40k from the old mark. The mark is set *before* the await, not after, because the subprocess is bounded at `PRUNE_TIMEOUT_MS` and two minutes of ticks would each find an unmarked run and spawn their own winnow at the same transcript. And the **depth is 3 while the chart is still depth 1**, which is the fifth: the stacked area draws one band per top-level provenance and nothing under it, from exactly the rows it drew from when the reading itself stopped at depth 1, but the two levels below — the tool or attachment class, then the artefact, a file path with its repeat count, a Bash command head, an MCP tool, one sub-agent's return — are read and stored for a detail view. They are stored on **different terms**, and the difference is the whole bound. The series stays one row per provenance per reading, because `CONTEXT_COMPOSITIONS_PER_RUN` counts *readings* and that argument only holds while a reading is a handful of rows. The tree goes to `context_composition_children`, for the **newest reading of a run only, replaced whole on each reading**, in the same transaction that writes the bands. That is what bounds the store by runs instead of by readings × paths: nothing on this path caps how many distinct files a run may touch, so a tree per reading is unbounded in the one dimension with no ceiling anywhere. The trade is stated rather than hidden — the tree answers "what is in this window **now**", which the newest reading is, and cannot answer what was in the third of forty. Within a reading, `COMPOSITION_CHILDREN_PER_NODE` caps a node's children at 64, largest first, and the tail is **dropped rather than pooled**: a subtree short of its parent is visible, where an "other" bin is a node indistinguishable from `unattributed`, whose whole job is to say what nothing accounted for. `parseComposition` lifts winnow's `×N` off an artefact label into its own field, because `decorate()` welds the two together and a view keyed on the composed string treats one file read three times and the same file read four as two different files; null there is "winnow attached no count", never "once". Measured 2026-09-04 against the pinned ref, a depth-3 `--json` body on this install's four largest transcripts — 7.2 MB to 12.9 MB of JSONL — came back at 18 KB to 29 KB with 72 to 110 sub-nodes and at most 29 under any one parent, so the 4 MB stdout bound `contextComposition` was written with keeps two orders of headroom and the per-node cap does not fire on anything here.

**Winnow's window and this app's are the same measure from different anchors, and nothing may subtract one from the other.** Both are `input + cache_creation + cache_read` on a priced request. `apiContextSample` anchors on the last **main-thread** `usage` frame, sidechains excluded exactly as the ceiling excludes them, because a sub-agent's turns are not this conversation's context; winnow anchors on the last priced request in the file whatever wrote it, and a sub-agent's frames are written to that same file. So the two agree on an idle conversation and come apart for exactly as long as a sub-agent runs — 22 minutes at a stretch on this install, the same stretch `noteContextCheck` exists for. `recordComposition` therefore stores **winnow's own** window on the row and the stack is drawn against it. Scaling the slices onto the sample's figure would make the two agree by construction and would be a lie in the one case it mattered: the bands would then apportion a total they were never measured against. The component no longer says which reading is which: that sentence was removed from the page at the operator's request, so the separation is stated here and in `CompositionStack`'s own header comment and nowhere the operator will read it. Nothing on the page now warns against the subtraction. A reading is written whole or not at all — one transaction, a row per band — because a half-written one is the stacked area's single unrecoverable state: the bands sum to less than the window and the gap reads as unattributed context rather than as a missing row. The cap is counted in **readings** and not rows for the same reason, and `parseComposition` drops a node it cannot read rather than zeroing it, since zero is a measurement and a short sum is visible where a false band is not. **The residual is signed, it is taken here against the window rather than copied off winnow, and the stack sizes its axis to the taller of the window and the bands.** Three decisions, and they were made together on 2026-09-10 because the first was being violated and the other two are what it costs to honour it. Winnow's residual is `window − Σ nodes + shed` — the shed being what left the window between two priced requests with no compaction boundary to explain it, an early-end cut or an interrupt, added back because winnow's rows describe what the transcript recorded *arriving* — and it is negative wherever the estimates over-explain the window, which winnow puts at a third of sessions and which was every reading of the run that surfaced this once it carried tool traffic. `parseComposition` floored every node at zero, the residual included, so the stored bands summed past their window on every such reading — 324,569 against 243,678 at the last one — and `CompositionStack`, sized to the window and clamping every running total at it on the promise that the bands sum to it, drew standing configuration, prefix and conversation with their top on their bottom from the reading where tool traffic and retained reasoning alone crossed the axis. Three provenances in the legend and absent from the picture, which read as tool traffic pushing the prefix out of the window: the one part of it nothing can move. So the residual here is `window − Σ provenances`, winnow's own stated derivation without the add-back, differing from its printed node by exactly the shed — measured directly, a printed −17,969 with 63,853 of shed added back against −81,822 taken here — and every *provenance* stays floored, which changes nothing winnow prints since it floors them itself and is what lets the stack treat every band but one as a height. The axis is the taller of the two at every reading, the excess from the window up to what the bands claim is drawn as a hatched strip in the residual's own fill over whichever bands it falls in, the residual's legend row wears the same stripes and a signed figure, and one caption, present only while there is a strip, says what it is. The rows already stored were repaired rather than left: `migrate()` takes every residual row again against its reading's window on every boot, idempotently, because the window and the provenances on a stored reading are exact copies of what winnow printed and the residual is a function of them alone.
